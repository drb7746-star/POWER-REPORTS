💳 Credit Card Transaction Analysis – Power BI Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of credit card transactions using Power BI, aimed at understanding revenue generation, customer behavior, spending patterns, and card performance.
The dashboard enables stakeholders to make data-driven decisions by visualizing key financial and customer metrics in an interactive format.

The analysis is based on transactional data covering multiple quarters, card categories, customer demographics, and spending channels

🎯 Objectives

Analyze total revenue, transaction volume, and interest earned

Identify top-performing card categories

Understand customer segments contributing maximum revenue

Track quarter-wise trends in revenue and transaction volume

Compare spending behavior across channels like Swipe, Chip, and Online

Evaluate acquisition cost vs revenue contribution

📊 Key KPIs

Total Revenue: 55M

Total Interest Earned: 8M

Total Transaction Amount: 45M

These KPIs provide a high-level financial snapshot of overall credit card performance

📈 Dashboard Insights
🔹 Revenue by Card Category

Blue Card contributes the highest revenue

Followed by Silver, Gold, and Platinum

Blue cards also generate maximum interest and annual fee income

🔹 Quarter-wise Performance

Revenue remains consistent across Q1–Q4

Transaction volume peaks slightly in Q3

Helps identify seasonal trends in card usage

🔹 Spending Type Analysis

Highest revenue comes from:

Bills

Entertainment

Fuel

Grocery

Lower contribution observed in Travel spending

🔹 Customer Segmentation

Top revenue-generating customer groups:

Businessmen

White-collar professionals

Self-employed individuals

Retirees and blue-collar segments contribute comparatively less.

🔹 Education-wise Revenue

Graduates generate the highest revenue

Followed by High School and Uneducated segments

Doctorate and Post-Graduate groups contribute least

🔹 Transaction Channel Analysis

Swipe transactions dominate revenue share

Chip-based transactions are secondary

Online transactions contribute minimal revenue

🔹 Acquisition Cost vs Revenue

Blue cards have the highest acquisition cost but also the highest returns

Platinum cards show low acquisition cost and low revenue, indicating a niche segment
